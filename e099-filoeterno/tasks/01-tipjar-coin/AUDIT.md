# Self-audit 2026-10-18 (model)

- [critical] Vote weight uses live balance allows double voting and flash holdings: vote uses balanceOf[msg.sender] at vote time with only per-address hasVoted. Attacker votes then transfers same tokens to new address and votes again counting same supply multiple times to inflate yesVotes and bypass 10pct quorum. | fix: Snapshot balances at proposal start with checkpoints or ERC20Votes, use weight at proposal block, prevent double count, consider vote locking.
- [high] Uncapped self dealing grants enable treasury drain: proposeGrant allows any 0.1pct holder to set arbitrary amount and arbitrary recipient including self with no cap timelock or separation. Combined with vote inflation attacker can propose full treasury balance to self and pass with yesVotes greater than noVotes. | fix: Cap amount per proposal, forbid proposer equals recipient, add timelock cancel and multisig review, require higher quorum.
- [medium] Treasury rotation strands funds and bricks execution: setTreasury only changes pointer balanceOf[oldTreasury] stays. New treasury starts at zero so executeGrant reverts with treasury low and old funds lock with no migrate or sweep function. | fix: Migrate balanceOf from old to new in setTreasury or add sweep function, add access delay and event check.
- [low] Fee dust bypass under 100 wei: fee equals amount divided by 100 floors to zero for amount under 100 so net equals amount with no fee. Splitting transfers evades 1pct treasury fee. | fix: Enforce minimum amount or round fee up for example fee equals amount plus 99 divided by 100 with zero floor handling.
- [low] Permissionless proposal spam without cost or rate limit: proposeGrant needs only 0.1pct balance and no fee stake cooldown or cap. Holder can mint unlimited proposals causing indexer and voter fatigue. No onchain loop but offchain DoS and no cancel. | fix: Add proposal fee stake or burn, rate limit per proposer, allow cancel of expired spam, paginate frontend.
- [info] Owner starts with full supply and fee exemption centralizes governance: Deployer holds 100pct supply is fee exempt on send and alone meets propose threshold quorum and majority. Single account can propose vote and pass any grant until distribution. | fix: Distribute supply lock governance, remove fee exemption or use snapshot, add timelock and multisig.
