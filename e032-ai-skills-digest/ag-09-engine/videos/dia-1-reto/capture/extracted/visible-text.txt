Reference module: [teaching-plan M1] — "De la idea al guion" — Dictate a topic on the phone; turn it into a scene-by-script with a free AI chat. Any AI chat (phone app). $0 [estimated].
Honesty rule: past is not future — prices measured/verified 2026-08-17/18, they change. No views/income promises.
Day 1 content: choose ONE thing you can explain in <2 min (un consejo, un error que cometiste, una explicación corta). Don't seek the perfect topic — the right topic is the one you can explain today without researching. Open your free AI chat on your phone and dictate your idea with your voice, as if talking to a friend. Then ask: "Convierte esto en un guion por escenas para un video de 1 minuto, en español sencillo, con una idea por escena." Cost: $0. Result: a finished scene-by-scene script — a written plan, not just an idea.
