## Video direction

**palette system** — Bold Poster: white `bg` sheet ground · `dark` ink text/borders · tomato `red`
is the ONLY accent (every numeral, eyebrow, leftbar, progress bar) · `light` off-white stripes.
Never a fifth color; red is never body text.

**motion grammar + reveal model** — long-tail eases (power3 default). Every frame reveals
piece-by-piece **on the voiceover's spoken cue**: at t=0 only what the VO is saying enters;
each numeral/word/stamp pops when spoken; end on a held read (stillness beats motion; subtle
jitter at most). No front-loading, no lazy breathing, no end-of-shot pans.

**rhythm / held frames** — Frames 1 (hook) and 8 (landing) are the held/breather bookends;
Frames 2–4 are the rapid cascade (each a single strong reveal, ~2 cues); Frame 5 (9/10) is the
turn — slower, two-part reveal, deliberate pause before the 2.38%; Frames 6–7 keep the cadence
up to the deadline.

**negative list** — no slideshow (front-load then freeze), no screensaver (everything floating),
no box-shadow (only the stacked text-shadow on red display), no rounded corners (square only),
no second accent, no generic "AI" purple-blue gradients.

**chrome** — red progress bar at the bottom edge of every frame, width grows with frame index.
