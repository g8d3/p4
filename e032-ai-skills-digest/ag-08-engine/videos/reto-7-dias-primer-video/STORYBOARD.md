# Storyboard — reto-7-dias-primer-video

**Strategy:** "This video performs the 7-day challenge it teaches: an AI Spanish
voice (no face) walks a non-technical audience through one action per day to
publish their first AI-made explainer video from their phone, honestly — costs
measured, day-4 phone assembly marked OPEN GAP, no money promises."

## Video direction

- **Palette system** (from `frame.md` — Capsule): `cream` ground `#F5F5F0` on
  every frame + atmosphere (1–3 radial candy glows 6–15% + 4% grain overlay).
  `ink` type `#1A1A1A`, universal **2px ink outline** on every pill/card.
  Candy accents (`coral`/`sky`/`lime`/`lavender`/`violet`/`yellow`/`peach`/
  `mint`) on **pill fills + stat numerals only — never headlines**. Soft
  hard-offset shadows (`0.2–0.4cqw`, 8% ink) on content pills only. Frames 00
  and 11 invert: `ink` ground with `cream` type — the dramatic bookends.
- **Typography** — Bodoni Moda 700–800 display, **ink, sentence case**,
  fit-to-measure (≤78cqw); Space Grotesk for body + uppercase tracked
  pill/label chrome. Hero words near full-bleed. Portrait: hero occupies the
  top ~70% of the 83% keep-out zone; captions own the bottom 17%.
- **Motion grammar** — long-tail settle (`power3`) everywhere; **reveals land
  on their spoken VO cue, spread across the shot — never front-loaded.** Holds
  read still; only subtle jitter on a held frame. No lazy breathing, no
  back-half pan/push. Only exits: final frame.
- **Rhythm** — day frames (03–09) open with the day pill ("DÍA 1"…) that
  springs in first, then the day's visual builds as the VO explains. Frames
  00/02/10/11/12 resolve to strong held reads.
- **Negative list** — no purple/blue "AI" gradients, no floating bokeh, no
  generic decorative shapes, no real browser chrome/cursors, no faces/cameras
  (faceless by contract), no slideshow failure (dump-then-freeze).
