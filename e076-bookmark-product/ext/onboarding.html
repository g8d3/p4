BookmarkVault is on

  1. 1Open x.com/i/bookmarks or x.com/i/history in this browser.
  2. 2Tap the BookmarkVault icon (puzzle menu → pin it first on mobile) to open the panel.
  3. 3Scroll normally. The panel's Saved tab fills live — that list on this device is where everything lands. The number you see is your saved count.
  4. 4To keep a copy outside your phone, open the panel's Sync tab, set server + token, tap Sync. Without that, everything stays only on this device.

New saves are captured the next time you open bookmarks. Full-history auto-import (human-paced scroll) is coming — the panel will say when.

Nothing to configure. Just browse.
