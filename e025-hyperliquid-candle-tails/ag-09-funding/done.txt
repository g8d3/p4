# ag-09-funding Analysis Verdicts

## Question 1: Does Crowding Predict Reversal?
**VERDICT:** NO EFFECT FOUND

**Evidence:**
- Extreme positive funding → +0.46% next-day return (positive, not negative)
- Extreme negative funding → +0.25% next-day return (also positive)
- No consistent pattern across coins (replication rate < 50%)
- 5-day horizon shows slight negative for extreme positive (-1.15%) but insufficient second-half sample

**Honest null:** Funding extremes do not reliably predict short-term reversals.

---

## Question 2: Does Funding Explain the Weekday Pattern?
**VERDICT:** NO EXPLANATION

**Evidence:**
- Funding rates uniform across weekdays (23-25 bps variation)
- Monday/Wednesday weakness exists independent of funding
- Correlation between funding level and weekday returns: near zero

**Honest null:** Weekday pattern is funding-independent; likely driven by other factors (liquidity, flows).

---

## Question 3: Does Extreme Funding Before Crashes Predict Reversion?
**VERDICT:** MIXED EVIDENCE, SMALL SAMPLE

**Evidence:**
- Crashes after extreme positive funding: +4.18% 5-day return (strong reversion)
- But only 30 such crashes vs 653 normal crashes
- Extreme negative pre-crash: -1.07% (worse, contrary to hypothesis)
- Sample size < 100 per bucket (insufficient per methodology)

**Honest null:** Hint of effect but underpowered; needs more data.

---

## Question 4: Does Funding Mean-Revert?
**VERDICT:** HIGHLY PERSISTENT

**Evidence:**
- 1-day autocorrelation: 0.5-0.8 (very high persistence)
- 30-day autocorrelation: 0.0-0.7 (varies; some coins persist)
- Extreme spells last avg 1.8-2.3 days (not hours)
- Max spell duration: 29 days (negative extremes)

**Honest null:** Funding is a slow regime indicator, not a fast timing signal.

---

## Overall Replication Verdict

**Per-coin replication rate:** < 50% for funding-reversal hypothesis
**Split-sample replication:** First half shows some patterns; second half underpowered
**Cross-question consistency:** No coherent story emerges

**Final assessment:** Funding sentiment shows strong persistence but limited predictive power for price direction in this dataset.
