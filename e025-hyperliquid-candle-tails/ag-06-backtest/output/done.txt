## Verdict

The 1d weekday pattern is **statistically real** (p<0.0001) but the strategy
built from it **fails out-of-sample**: Rule A loses money even before fees,
because the spec trades the **same-day intraday** while the ag-05 finding
measured the **next-day** return (empirically ret_next[w] ≈ intraday[w+1]).
The pattern lives one weekday to the side of where the spec points. Fixing
the rule post-hoc was deliberately avoided (data snooping). All deliverables
exist: permutation_test.md, strategy_spec.md, backtest.csv, backtest_report.md,
backtest_equity.png, beginners_guide.md, session-log.md.
