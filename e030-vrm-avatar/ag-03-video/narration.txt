Hi there! Let me introduce you to something special. I'm a VRM avatar, a 3D character built with Three.js and the Three-VRM library. What makes me unique? I'm not just a static model — I can be controlled programmatically through a simple JSON API. Want me to smile? Change my expression. Want me to look somewhere? Set a target. Want me to speak? I can lip-sync to any audio you provide. Behind the scenes, a WebSocket server on port 8787 handles real-time commands. Two different clients, same protocol, different characters. We use KIE TTS for narration, Parakeet ASR for transcription, and ffmpeg to extract mouth movements from the audio energy. This system is built for talking-head videos — captured at 608 by 1080, encoded with VAAPI for GPU acceleration. Best of all? Everything's driven by code, not manual clicks. That means automated, reproducible character performances. Pretty cool, right?
