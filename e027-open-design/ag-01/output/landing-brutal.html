V. Method / Loop • 04 stages, iterative 005 / 008
Method · Nº 05

FROM PROBES TO PRODUCTS.

+

Every stage is iterative, visual, and research-driven — verifiable artifacts, not opaque prompts.

01

PROBE →

Run the detection harness against the target surface. Record every divergence and every delta.

02

MASK →

Patch fingerprints at the network, TLS, canvas, and WebGL layers until the probe cannot tell.

03

RENDER →

Point the agent at the brief — it shoots, captions, and edits video end-to-end, logging every cut.

04

SHIP

Publish the harness and benchmark alongside the artifact so any claim re-runs on any box.

Research informs everything. Files make it real.
