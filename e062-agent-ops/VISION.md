# VISION — the wheel that spins forever (owner 2026-09-14)

The owner opens his phone, any app, and it is finished. Tables everywhere —
every list, every database with all its relations, nested and tappable.
Nothing loose, no paragraphs lying around, every long text one plain line
plus a tap. Columns fit the phone because the AI laid them out, not the
owner. Every number carries its time. He understands it in under 30 seconds
with no explanation, and it does one more money-relevant thing than
yesterday: one more data column, one more grade, one more suggestion
that earns.

Behind that, agents work continuously, not in half-hour boxes. One finishes,
the next is born with the full thread and zero weight — context fills,
answers slow, so fresh agents, compressed start, no baggage. The 30-minute
cron is training wheels, tolerated today for two reasons only: credits are
almost-but-not infinite, and the owner needs rest between reviews. The
target is the chain that never sleeps yet only taps the owner when a proof
number moves — record-slip style, score plus velocity. Everything else stays
on the board, drillable.

It never breaks what works: BEST stays stable while NEXT dares. Old blocks
never interrupt the new one. Structure exists — versions, freshness, no
stale — but it is invisible scaffolding, never the headline. No specs, no
suggestions, no theater. Either the phone surprises the owner, or the leg
failed.

A machine that runs like a miner, earns its next leg, and lets the owner do
more business from his phone while needing to know less.
