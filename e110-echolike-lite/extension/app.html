Why it is not automatic

Scroll-walking someone's history on a timer would look exactly like a scraper and cost you X rate limits. A button you press, on a tab you are already looking at, keeps it a deliberate act — and the extension stops after your count, or when the list stops growing, or after ten minutes.

Imports are deduped by post: importing twice, or importing posts you already captured live, adds nothing. The quota (3,000 a month) still applies, so a very large import may stop at the limit with an honest message.
