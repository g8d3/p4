P12 · what a space is

A space is a list several people write to. Every capture shows how many people in the space saved the same post — that is how the network finds itself, without anyone publishing a profile. Joining is by an invite: one code you decide the uses and lifetime of (3 people, 24 hours by default), and that you can revoke at any moment.

P6 · what to import

Why it is not automatic

Scroll-walking someone's history on a timer would look exactly like a scraper and cost you X rate limits. A button you press, on a tab you are already looking at, keeps it a deliberate act — and the extension stops after your count, or when the list stops growing, or after ten minutes.

Imports are deduped by post: importing twice, or importing posts you already captured live, adds nothing. The quota (3,000 a month) still applies, so a very large import may stop at the limit with an honest message.

P8 · your inbox code

2 · Your inbox code

One code, one list. This code is your inbox: where it lives, how to reach it, and the key that opens it. Paste it on another phone to share the list; keep it to recover everything. It works as a password — whoever holds it can read your captures.

P10 · account and key backup

4 · Account & key backup (optional)

An account is only for getting your inbox back on a new phone. Sign in with your email — no password — then back up your key wrapped with a passphrase you choose. The backup travels encrypted: our inbox stores it and cannot open it.
