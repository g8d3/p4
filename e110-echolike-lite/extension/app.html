2 · Recovery code

The key to everything in your inbox, and the only copy. It decrypts your captures — nobody can regenerate it, read it or recover it for you. Save it where you keep passwords.

An account is only for getting your inbox back on a new phone. Sign in with your email — no password — then back up your key wrapped with a passphrase you choose. The backup travels encrypted: our inbox stores it and cannot open it.
