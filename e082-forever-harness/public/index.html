📖 Plain words — what the jargon means

one line each, no manual.
checkpointsaved moment you can rewind to. compactiontidying memory: old ticks folded into one summary. criticsecond checker that tries to reject every tick. evidenceproof of real work attached to each tick. gatessafety checks each tick must pass. ledgerspend log: every tick's simulated cost. heartbeatthe loop's pulse; a stale pulse means stuck. policyyour instructions to the loop. qualityhealth score: 100 minus 15 per failed check. restorerewind to a saved moment (adds, never rewrites). stress testattack test we run against ourselves. visitorsvisit counts: who came, who got stuck. backlogto-do list the loop works through. mintmake a key (token). thresholdtrip point where a verdict flips. botrobot visit (our own tests), hidden by default. forevernever stops on tick count (money can still stop it).
