About SnapConvert

Built by an independent founder who kept losing time copying text out of PDFs. SnapConvert does one job: turn a PDF into clean text in seconds, right in your browser, with no upload.

Why built: most converters force an upload, an account, and a wait. SnapConvert extracts text locally with pdf.js, so small jobs stay private and instant. Pro funds hosting and batch mode.

Contact: support@snapconvert.site — monitored daily, replies within 2 business days. Include your order ID for billing issues.
