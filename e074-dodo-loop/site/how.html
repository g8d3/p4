How SnapConvert works

  1. Step 1 — Pick a PDF. On the home page, click the file input and choose any text-based PDF. Or try our sample.pdf first.
  2. Step 2 — Text is extracted in your browser. pdf.js reads the file locally and shows the first 3 pages as plain text. Nothing is uploaded; large or scanned-image PDFs may return no text.
  3. Step 3 — Copy or upgrade. Copy the text from the output box. Free covers 5 conversions a month; Pro ($6/mo) or Lifetime ($49) unlocks full-length and batch conversion.

Limits: text-based PDFs only (no OCR for scans yet), preview shows the first 3 pages, files stay on your device.
